About VelvetVerse ✦

VelvetVerse is a story universe for every kind of imagination: dark romance, fantasy, mystery, thriller, teen drama, supernatural tales, and everything in between. Open a book, step into another world, and follow the secrets hidden between the pages.

To let everyone on the internet share stories, likes, and comments, this website will need to be published online and connected to a secure database. The current version is a working local prototype.
